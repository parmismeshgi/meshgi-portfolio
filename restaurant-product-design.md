Role evidence

## The work matches the full product cycle.

Each point below links to the case study with the strongest proof.

01 · Research

### Talk with restaurant teams

Bar Tabs includes interviews with 15 waitstaff and managers, customer sessions, competitive research, Smart Serve training, and work behind a bar.

[See the field research →](https://meshgi.com/bar-tabs/#decisions)

02 · Prototype

### Make ideas testable early

Kiosk quantity controls moved through five concepts, eight refinements, paper tests, hardware trials, two voting rounds, and one shipped rule set.

[See the Kiosk test →](https://meshgi.com/kiosk/#decisions)

03 · Delivery

### Stay through implementation

POS and Backoffice work records responsive rules, component behavior, engineering handoff, staged releases, and build review.

[See shipped platform patterns →](https://meshgi.com/pos/#product)

04 · Product depth

### Learn the operating system

The work connects POS, Kiosk, Bar Tabs, and Backoffice. Each decision accounts for permissions, hardware, service states, and connected workflows.

[Compare the four products →](https://meshgi.com/product-design/#projects)

05 · AI

### Use AI inside the work

I use connected sources to synthesize research, compare concepts, check current product rules, build prototypes, and preserve decisions.

[Read the AI practice →](https://meshgi.com/ai-design-practice/)

06 · Systems

### Return patterns to the system

The shared POS keyboard, responsive guidance, Menu Management components, and navigation rules reduce repeated design and engineering decisions.

[See the component decisions →](https://meshgi.com/backoffice/#decisions)

07 · Critique

### Show unfinished work

The Quick Menu record keeps rejected directions, critique notes, and the reason version six won. The case studies explain tradeoffs, limits, and open measures.

[See the iteration record →](https://meshgi.com/backoffice/#decisions)

08 · Shipping

### Separate release from intent

Every case study labels released, partial, proposed, and unmeasured work. Kiosk and POS include dated production footprints.

[See the release evidence →](https://meshgi.com/kiosk/#result)
