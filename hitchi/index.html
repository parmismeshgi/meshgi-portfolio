Project value

Turn music memory into a shared timeline game.

What the design needed to achieve

  • ✓

    Keep song details hidden until the player commits to a guess.

  • ✓

    Connect physical cards to a fast mobile scan loop.

  • ✓

    Support Persian and English players in the same room.

Problem

Playback can spoil the game.

Music services can reveal the song, artist, artwork, or year before the guess.

Risk

Digital can replace the table.

A heavy app can pull attention away from cards, debate, and shared memory.

Direction

Use the phone as a bridge.

Let the card start playback, protect the answer, and return attention to the group.

Design decisions

Protect the surprise across cards, sound, and screen.

01

Hide answer metadata

Spoiler control

The pre-reveal scan response hides title, artist, year, artwork, and other answer clues.

Intended impact

Preserve the guess even when playback begins on a phone.

02

Keep the turn loop physical

Table-first play

The player scans, listens, places the card in time, and then reveals the answer.

Intended impact

Use the screen for support while the shared timeline stays central.

03

Design for two reading directions

Bilingual play

The experience supports Persian and English. The Persian timeline places older music on the right and newer music on the left.

Intended impact

Keep the game natural for mixed-language groups.

04

Separate preview from release

Honest status

The working scanner and website remain a preview while catalog approval, rights, and print checks stay open.

Intended impact

Show the product without presenting candidate material as release-ready.

Product proof

The current preview carries one clear turn from scan to reveal.

Result

A complete product direction now connects the card and scanner.

Working evidence

Playable preview

The current package includes the game rules, card system, scanner flow, and bilingual website.

Verified boundary

Release gates stay open

Catalog, print, privacy, and music-rights checks still block a final public release.

Measure next

Table play

Test scan success, time to first song, spoiler failures, rule questions, and replay. Public results are not available.
