## How I work

### The screen comes after the questions.

I start with the problem. I study the system, speak with its users, explore directions, and test the strongest one.

- Understand the problem and the people affected by it.

- Find the system limits and connected workflows.

- Explore several approaches before choosing a direction.

- Test the design and use the findings to refine it.

- Explain the decision, support delivery, and review the build.

AI speeds up research and production. Design judgment, source checks, and final decisions stay with the team.
