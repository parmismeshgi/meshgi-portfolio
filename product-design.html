How I work

The screen comes after the questions.

I start with the problem. I study the system, speak with its users, explore directions, and test the strongest one.

  1. Understand the problem and the people affected by it.
  2. Find the system limits and connected workflows.
  3. Explore several approaches before choosing a direction.
  4. Test the design and use the findings to refine it.
  5. Explain the decision, support delivery, and review the build.

AI speeds up research and production. Design judgment, source checks, and final decisions stay with the team.

Let’s make the hard part feel simple.
