Project value

Make location history useful without trading away privacy.

What the design needed to achieve

  • ✓

    Record a day with little manual work.

  • ✓

    Turn raw location points into a readable personal story.

  • ✓

    Keep ownership clear through local storage and direct export.

Problem

Memory takes effort.

Manual check-ins interrupt the day. Most people will not build a complete location journal by hand.

Risk

Location reveals routines.

A useful record can also expose home, work, travel, and repeated habits.

Direction

Automate with restraint.

The product should collect enough context to help, while keeping the record on the device.

Design decisions

Connect each design choice to its intended value.

01

Remove manual check-ins

Less input effort

Footsteps records visits and movement in the background. The user does not rebuild the day by hand.

Intended impact

More complete days with fewer repeated actions.

02

Turn tracking into a timeline

Faster recall

Places, travel modes, duration, and maps form one chronological story.

Intended impact

Users can understand where time went without reading raw location data.

03

Explain privacy before permission

Clearer trust

The opening flow explains local storage, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no account.

Intended impact

People see the privacy boundary before granting sensitive access.

04

Keep the record portable

Data ownership

CSV and JSON exports let people take places, visits, and activities elsewhere.

Intended impact

The journal remains useful outside the app.

Product proof

The released screens carry the same value story.

Result

A focused utility reached the App Store.

Released evidence

Public iOS product

Footsteps launched for iPhone and iPad on July 21, 2026.

Verified boundary

Local-only privacy

The public product states no accounts, cloud sync, analytics, tracking, or ads.

Measure next

Value signals

Track complete days, correction rates, battery impact, exports, and repeat use. Public results are not yet available.
